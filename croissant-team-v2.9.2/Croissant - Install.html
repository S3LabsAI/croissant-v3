Croissant v2.9.2

A Chrome extension for your GOAL accounts: budget pace alerts, conversion flags, a source review with bid raises you approve, client notes and Friday summaries, and a live panel in the corner of every GOAL page.

Made for your accounts

It tracks your Active accounts in GOAL: the same list as the admin Clients page filtered to Status: Active and Account Manager: you. It works out who you are from your GOAL admin sign-in, reads each account's budgets live every morning, and shows the count in the totals row ("N active accounts") so you can check it against GOAL.

Install (about 2 minutes)

  1. Unzip budget-pace-alerts-v2.9.2.zip somewhere it can stay (Chrome runs it from there).
  2. In Chrome, open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the Croissant folder you unzipped.
  4. Pin the croissant from the puzzle-piece icon, then open the GOAL admin dashboard once to connect it.
  5. Click the croissant, open Settings, and under Client notes put your name in Sign-off.
If the account count doesn't match GOAL, put your Account Manager ID in Settings (the number after "value" in the Clients page link once you filter to yourself). New versions install themselves when their files are copied into the same folder.

What it does

Corner panelThe full view sits flush in the bottom-right of every GOAL page. Minimize it to a pill with today's key numbers.
Budget pace alerts25, 50, 75 and 100% of each day's budget, projected cap times, and a heads-up when an account will cap early.
Daily budget snapshotReads every account's budgets from GOAL each morning. Changes nothing in GOAL.
FlagsNo spend, behind pace, conversion drops and no-lead runs, tuned to about 1 to 2 false alarms a week.
Sources reviewShows which source caused a drop and proposes +10% on the best converters (25 points max). Nothing changes until you click Apply.
Client notes80%+ conversion or 2+ inbound calls drafts a short, friendly email to the owner. You send it.
Friday summariesUpbeat weekly recap per account, with a link to its Vercel report site.
Briefing and check-ins8am briefing, plus a check-in draft when an account hasn't heard from you in 14 days.
Best hoursWhich hours each account converts best, for client conversations.
Daily CSV and SlackEach day's report saved to Downloads/GOAL Pace; optional Slack DMs and a 5pm recap.

Never changes daily limits or base bids. Source bid changes happen only after you approve them.
